    Hi there! I'm David Dong, and welcome to my little corner of the web.

    I live and work in Beijing, China :cn: , working as a software developer for a semiconductor company abroad.

    On this blog, I write about whatever's on my mind — which usually ends up being about design and programming, since that's what I do day to day. I genuinely enjoy helping people pick up new skills and ideas, and this blog is my way of giving back.

    Outside of work, I'm passionate about travel. I love exploring different landscapes, experiencing new cultures, and seeing how people live around the world. I've collected some of my travel stories and photos in my Album — feel free to take a look.

    If you'd like to get in touch, feel free to drop me an email. You can also find me on LinkedIn or GitHub — I check both regularly.

    about this site
